Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful travel tips before your journey to Ghana.

Most international passport holders require a visa prior to departure or an official Visa on Arrival / eVisa authorization. Your passport must have at least 6 months validity. Upon booking, Kelrose Tours issues official stamped confirmation letters required for embassy or online visa processing.

Yes, an international Yellow Fever card is mandatory for all travelers over 9 months of age to enter Ghana. Port health officials verify this at Kotoka International Airport. We also advise consulting a travel clinic regarding malaria prophylaxis.

The local currency is the Ghana Cedi (GHS). We accept online card payments, bank transfers, and local cash. ATMs are widely available across Accra and Kumasi. Foreign exchange bureaus offer good rates for clean US Dollars, Euros, and British Pounds.

Ghana is a wonderful year-round tropical destination! The dry months from November to March are ideal for beach days and seeing wild elephants in Mole National Park. December is also famous for major festive cultural events and music festivals.
